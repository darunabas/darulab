Evolutionary biogeography and ecology · Stanford

Why plants live where they do, and how to protect them.

We combine herbarium collections, phylogenetics, genomics and biodiversity informatics to explain plant distributions and set conservation priorities, working in the field, the herbarium, the wet lab and on high-performance computing clusters.
